My research lies at the intersection of **String Theory**, **Conformal Field Theory**, and **Quantum Gravity**. I am particularly interested in exact worldsheet descriptions of string theory in curved spacetimes, and the mathematical structures underlying these theories.

### Current Research: Worldsheet String Theory and BTZ Black Hole Thermodynamics

My current research focuses on understanding the **partition function and entropy of the BTZ black hole from a worldsheet string theory perspective**, within the framework of the AdS₃/CFT₂ correspondence.

The primary objective is to derive a worldsheet string theory expression for the BTZ black hole partition function and subsequently extract its thermodynamic entropy. The investigation begins with string propagation in an AdS₃ background supported by NS–NS flux, described by the **SL(2,ℝ) Wess–Zumino–Witten (WZW) model**. This involves studying the nonlinear sigma model, the background metric and antisymmetric B-field, and the associated worldsheet conformal field theory.

A central challenge is understanding how the BTZ black hole geometry, obtained as a quotient of AdS₃, is encoded in the worldsheet theory and how its thermal partition function can be constructed consistently. The ultimate goal is to recover the **Bekenstein–Hawking entropy of the BTZ black hole**, investigate possible string-theoretic corrections, and explore their relationship with holography and two-dimensional conformal field theory.

### Departmental Project 1

My Departmental Project 1 work focused on the exact worldsheet description of string propagation on the AdS₃ manifold using the **SL(2,ℝ) Wess–Zumino–Witten (WZW) model**.

The primary topics I am currently studying include

- Classical Trajectories
- Affine Kac–Moody algebras and Sugawara construction
- Virasoro constraints
- Spectral flow symmetry
- Representation theory of strings in AdS₃
- Physical Spectrum of strings in AdS₃
- Bosonization and Scattering amplitudes of strings in AdS₃

These studies aim to provide a deeper understanding of string dynamics in curved spacetimes and their connection to holography through the AdS/CFT correspondence.

### Previous Research

During my Master's research, I worked on problems in black hole physics and gravitational perturbation theory.

The primary topics included

- Black hole surrounded by an anisotropic matter field
- Quasinormal Modes through Scalar, EM and Gravitational Perturbations
- Black shadow radius and Lyapunov exponent
- Greybody Factors
- Eikonal Approximation
